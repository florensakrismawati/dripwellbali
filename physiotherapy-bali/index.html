Physiotherapy Session

Contact for Quote

Comprehensive mobile physical therapy combining advanced electro-physical modalities and manual techniques to accelerate deep tissue healing, alleviate acute muscle spasm, and eliminate joint pain.

Therapy Modalities & Benefits:

  • • Ultrasound Therapy: Uses high-frequency sound waves to penetrate deep into muscle tissues, tendons, and ligaments. Stimulates micro-circulation, breaks down scar tissue, and speeds up soft tissue repair.
  • • Transcutaneous Electrical Nerve Stimulation (TENS): Delivers mild electrical impulses to block pain signals sent to the brain, stimulate endorphin production, and immediately soothe chronic or acute muscular pain.
  • • Infrared Lamp Therapy: Provides deep thermal warmth to relax stiff muscle fibers, increase local oxygenated blood flow, and relieve joint tension and back stiffness.
