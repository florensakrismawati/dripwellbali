Safety First Advisory

When You Need Emergency Hospital Evaluation

Seek immediate hospital emergency room evaluation if flu symptoms are accompanied by:

  • Severe difficulty breathing, wheezing, or shortness of breath
  • Persistent chest pain or feeling of pressure in lungs
  • High fever (>40°C / 104°F) that fails to come down
  • Sudden dizziness, confusion, or inability to stay awake
  • Inability to swallow fluids due to throat swelling
  • Bluish or pale color in lips or face
