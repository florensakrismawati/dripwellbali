Hangover in Bali: What Helps, When an IV Drip Makes Sense, and Warning Signs

By Dripwell Bali Medical Team • Medically reviewed by dr. Florensa Krismawati, S.Ked • Updated: November 2026 • 6 Min Read

A hangover is your body recovering from alcohol: dehydration, poor sleep and an irritated stomach all add up. In Bali, heat and sun make it worse. Most hangovers ease within a day with fluids, food and rest. There is no instant cure, but if you are vomiting or badly dehydrated, a doctor's visit with IV fluids can help. One thing matters more than anything else on this page: know the warning signs of methanol poisoning.

Why hangovers feel worse in Bali

  • Heat and sun: you lose more fluid through sweat, so you start the night partly dehydrated.
  • Long days: beach clubs that start in the afternoon often mean more drinks over more hours.
  • Little sleep: late nights and early sun cut sleep short, which makes headaches and tiredness worse.
  • Stomach upsets: cocktails with ice and late-night food can add a stomach bug on top. If diarrhoea is the main problem, read our Bali belly guide.

What actually helps

  1. Fluids, little and often. Water or oral rehydration solution (oralit, sold at pharmacies and minimarkets) in small sips every few minutes.
  2. Something simple to eat once you can: toast, crackers, bananas, rice or clear soup.
  3. Rest out of the sun. Air conditioning and sleep help more than a swim in the heat.
  4. No more alcohol. A "hair of the dog" drink only delays the hangover.
  5. Be careful with painkillers. Do not take them on an empty stomach, and ask a pharmacist or doctor which one is safe for you after heavy drinking.

Does an IV drip help a hangover?

Honestly: no treatment cures a hangover, and an IV drip is not a magic fix. What it can do is replace fluids quickly when you are dehydrated, and give medicine for nausea when vomiting stops you keeping water down. That is when a drip makes the most sense.

At Dripwell Bali, a doctor checks you before any drip and tells you if you do not need one. If you can drink normally, oral fluids and rest may be enough. See our Hangover Package for what the drip includes.

Methanol poisoning: know the warning signs

This is an emergency. Go to a hospital immediately if, after drinking, you or a friend have:

  • Blurred, changed or lost vision, or difficulty looking at bright lights
  • Breathlessness or fast breathing that gets worse
  • Severe stomach pain, vomiting, dizziness or confusion
  • Feeling much worse than others who drank as much

Cases of methanol poisoning from alcoholic drinks have been reported in Bali. Methanol poisoning can look like a bad hangover at first, and symptoms can take around 12 hours to appear. Do not wait for a villa visit or an IV drip: specific hospital treatment is needed, and speed matters.

Lower your risk: drink at reputable venues, choose sealed bottled drinks and well-known brands, be wary of very cheap cocktails and spirits, avoid homemade or unlabelled arak, and never leave your drink unattended.

When to see a doctor or go to hospital

Call a doctor (we can come to your villa or hotel) if you cannot keep fluids down, you are dizzy when you stand, you pass very little urine, or you are not improving after a day.

Go to a hospital emergency department for any methanol warning sign above, or if someone is very hard to wake, has a seizure, slow or irregular breathing, chest pain, vomits blood, or has hit their head.

Before your next night out

  • Drink water before you go out and a glass between drinks
  • Eat a proper meal before drinking
  • Stay in the shade and take breaks from the afternoon sun
  • Know your limits, and watch out for your friends
  • Keep oralit in your villa for the morning after

Hangover in Bali: common questions

What is the fastest way to get over a hangover in Bali?

There is no instant cure. Drink water or oral rehydration solution in small, frequent sips, eat something simple, rest out of the sun and avoid more alcohol. Most hangovers ease within a day.

Does an IV drip cure a hangover?

No treatment cures a hangover. An IV drip can help when you are dehydrated or vomiting so much that you cannot keep fluids down, because the fluids go straight into the blood. A doctor should check you first, and if you can drink normally, oral fluids often work too.

How long does a hangover last?

A hangover usually eases within 24 hours. If you feel worse instead of better, or symptoms last longer than a day, see a doctor.

Can a hangover be methanol poisoning?

Sometimes. Methanol poisoning can look like a bad hangover at first, and symptoms can take around 12 hours to appear. Blurred or changed vision, breathlessness, severe stomach pain, confusion or feeling much worse than your friends who drank the same are warning signs. Go to a hospital emergency department immediately.
