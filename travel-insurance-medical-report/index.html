Medical Report for Travel Insurance Claims

Got sick in Bali and want to claim on your travel insurance? Our Bali Belly, Fever and Flu Relief packages include the documents most travel insurers ask for: a confidential medical report in English, signed and stamped by a licensed doctor, and an itemized receipt.

With these packages there is no extra charge for the documents. Just tell our team when you book that you plan to make a claim. With the other IV drip packages (Hangover, Jetlag, Energy Booster and the Immune Boosters), the same documents are available on request at no extra cost.

What you receive

Documents for Your Travel Insurance Claim

Confidential medical report

On Dripwell Bali letterhead and written in English. It covers your details, symptoms and history, vital signs, physical examination, diagnosis, the treatment given (IV fluids and medications with doses), take-home medication and the doctor's recommendations.

Doctor's signature and stamp

Each report is dated, signed and stamped by the licensed doctor, with the doctor's practice licence number (SIP) on the stamp.

Itemized invoice and receipt

Every charge listed separately, so your insurer can see what was paid for.

Lab results

Copies of any lab or rapid test results, if tests were done.

Step by step

How to Claim With Your Travel Insurance

  1. Tell us when you book. Mention on WhatsApp that you plan to claim on your travel insurance.
  2. Call your insurer's assistance line. Many policies ask you to let them know about medical treatment as soon as you can.
  3. Receive your documents. The doctor prepares the signed medical report and itemized receipt for you at the end of the visit.
  4. Keep copies. Take clear photos or scans of every document before you send them.
  5. Submit your claim. Send the documents with your insurer's claim form, usually online.

Questions About Travel Insurance Documents

Is the medical report included in the package price?

Yes. It is included in the Bali Belly, Fever and Flu Relief packages. With the Hangover, Jetlag, Energy Booster and Immune Booster packages, we can prepare the same documents on request at no extra cost. Just tell our team when you book.

Will my travel insurance accept the report?

Most travel insurers ask for a signed medical report and an itemized receipt, which is what we provide. Whether a claim is approved, and how much is reimbursed, is decided by your insurer and the terms of your policy, so please check your policy or call your insurer's assistance line.

Is the report in English?

Yes. Reports and receipts are written in English for international travel insurers.

When do I receive the documents?

Tell the team when you book that you plan to claim on your travel insurance. The documents are prepared for you at the end of the visit.

Do I pay first and claim later?

Usually yes. For mobile doctor visits most insurers work on a pay-and-claim basis: you pay for the treatment and then submit the documents to your insurer for reimbursement.

Can I get a report for a visit in Canggu, Seminyak, Uluwatu or Ubud?

Yes. The documents are available wherever you are staying in Bali.
