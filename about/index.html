```
02 — Who we are

A different way
to receive care.

Dripwell Bali is a home doctor and medical service designed for people who need convenient access to professional care while staying in Bali.

Whether you are recovering from Bali belly, feeling unwell with fever or flu, dealing with dehydration, looking for IV drip therapy, or needing physiotherapy, our service is designed to make the process simple: tell us what you need, share your location, and our medical team comes to you.

We believe receiving medical attention should feel calm, clear and personal. Instead of spending time travelling to a clinic or waiting in a crowded room, you can receive appropriate care in the comfort of your hotel or villa.

```

```
Our approach

Professional care,
without the waiting room.

Travelling in Bali should give you time to rest, explore and enjoy your surroundings. When illness interrupts your plans, however, finding medical care can feel stressful.

Dripwell Bali was created around a simple idea: bring medical care closer to the patient. Our home visit service allows you to communicate with our team, arrange a suitable visit, and receive medical attention where you are staying.

Every treatment should begin with an appropriate medical consultation. Our focus is not simply on delivering a treatment, but on understanding the situation and providing care responsibly.
```
