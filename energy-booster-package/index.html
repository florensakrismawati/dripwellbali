Safety First Advisory

When You Need Emergency Evaluation

Energy IV drips are designed for routine fatigue and rehydration. Seek immediate emergency evaluation if lethargy is accompanied by:

  • Chest pain, pressure, or irregular heart palpitations
  • Severe difficulty breathing or shortness of breath
  • Sudden weakness, facial numbness, or confusion
  • High unexplained fever (>39°C / 102°F)
  • Persistent fainting, blackout, or extreme dizziness
  • Severe abdominal pain or inability to retain fluids
