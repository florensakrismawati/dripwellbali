Why We See So Much Bali Belly in Canggu

Canggu is one of the areas where our team treats the most Bali belly patients. Many of them are international travellers, surfers and digital nomads who have been in Bali for a few days to a few weeks. The pattern is usually the same: a busy few days of eating out, then sudden diarrhoea, stomach cramps or vomiting.

Common triggers in Canggu

  • Eating out for every meal at different cafés, warungs and beach clubs
  • Raw salads, smoothie bowls, fresh juices and drinks with ice
  • Brushing teeth or rinsing fruit with tap water in a villa
  • Swallowing seawater while surfing, especially after heavy rain, when runoff reaches beaches such as Batu Bolong and Echo Beach
  • Sharing food at a villa party or group dinner, so several guests fall ill together

Lowering your risk while staying in Canggu

  • Drink bottled or filtered water, and use it to brush your teeth
  • Wash or sanitise your hands before eating
  • Be careful with raw food and ice if your stomach is sensitive
  • Consider skipping a surf for a day or two after heavy rain
  • Keep oral rehydration salts (oralit, sold at local pharmacies and minimarkets) in your villa

Recovering in your Canggu villa

For mild Bali belly, sip oral rehydration solution often, rest, and eat small portions of simple food such as rice, toast, bananas or plain soup once you feel able to. Avoid alcohol and strong coffee until you have recovered. Call us if you cannot keep fluids down, the symptoms keep going, or you feel dizzy and weak. Questions About Bali Belly Treatment in Canggu

Why is Bali belly so common in Canggu?

Canggu is one of the areas where our team treats the most Bali belly patients. Many visitors eat out at different cafés, warungs and beach clubs every day, enjoy raw food, smoothie bowls and drinks with ice, and surf in water that can carry runoff after heavy rain. Any of these can expose a stomach that is not used to local bacteria.

When can I surf again after Bali belly?

Wait until the diarrhoea and vomiting have stopped and you are eating and drinking normally again. Surfing in the sun while you are still dehydrated can make you feel faint. If you are unsure, ask our doctor during your follow-up.

How fast can you reach me in Canggu?

Arrival time depends on your exact location and traffic, so our team confirms an estimated time on WhatsApp once you send your address.

Will I need antibiotics?

Not always. Many cases of Bali Belly improve with rehydration and rest. The doctor assesses your symptoms and decides whether medication such as an antibiotic is needed.

When should I go to hospital instead?

Go to a hospital emergency department if you have blood in your stool, a high fever that does not come down, severe abdominal pain, confusion, fainting, or you cannot keep any fluids down for many hours.
