01 — Home Doctor Bali

Medical care.
Made easier.

Feeling unwell while travelling in Bali can be stressful. Finding transportation, waiting at a clinic or leaving your villa may be the last thing you want to do.

Dripwell Bali brings medical care closer to you. Our home doctor service allows you to request a medical visit at your hotel, villa or residence, subject to location and medical team availability.

Every visit begins with a medical assessment so our medical team can understand your condition and recommend appropriate care.

02 — What we can help with

Home doctor
services.

01 — CONSULTATION

Doctor Consultation

Discuss your symptoms and health concerns with a medical professional at your hotel, villa or residence.

02 — ASSESSMENT

General Medical Assessment

Our medical team can assess common non-emergency health concerns and advise you on appropriate next steps.

03 — FEVER

Fever Care

Medical assessment for fever and related symptoms, with treatment recommendations based on your condition.

04 — FLU & COLD

Flu & Cold

Medical consultation for common cold, influenza-like symptoms and other non-emergency respiratory complaints.

05 — BALI BELLY

Bali Belly

Assessment and supportive medical care for common gastrointestinal symptoms such as diarrhoea, vomiting and stomach discomfort.

06 — FOLLOW-UP

Medical Follow-Up

If appropriate, our medical team can advise you on follow-up care or whether further medical attention is required.
