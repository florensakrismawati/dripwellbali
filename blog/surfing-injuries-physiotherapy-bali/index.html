Common Surfing Injuries in Bali & How Physiotherapy Helps

Bali is globally renowned for world-class surf breaks like Uluwatu, Echo Beach, and Padang Padang. However, hours of paddling, high-impact wipeouts, and sharp shallow reefs can take a heavy toll on a surfer's body.

Paddling puts continuous biomechanical stress on your shoulders and spine, while pop-ups require explosive hip and knee mobility. At DripWell Bali, our licensed physical therapists provide targeted, mobile physiotherapy treatments directly at your villa or hotel to get you back in the water safely.

Most Common Surfing Injuries in Bali

1. "Surfer's Shoulder" (Rotator Cuff Impingement)

Paddling requires repetitive overhead shoulder extension. Over time, the rotator cuff tendons become inflamed and pinched between shoulder bones, causing sharp anterior shoulder pain during and after surfing sessions.

2. Lumbar Spine Strain (Lower Back Stiffness)

Lying in a hyperextended arch position while paddling strains the spinal erectae muscles and compresses lumbar facet joints. This often results in dull aching, tightness, and reduced back mobility.

3. Knee & Ankle Sprains

Executing sharp turns, landing heavy aerials, or twisting during a wipeout can strain knee collateral ligaments (MCL/LCL) and cause acute lateral ankle sprains.

4. Reef Cuts & Muscle Contusions

Striking shallow coral reefs during a wipeout can result in painful soft tissue contusions, deep bruising, and localized inflammation.

🌊 Why Professional Physical Therapy Matters

Ignoring minor muscle strains can cause chronic biomechanical compensations, leading to long-term ligament damage or tendonitis that halts your surf trip completely.

How Mobile Physiotherapy Accelerates Recovery

1. Manual Soft Tissue Mobilization

Targeted deep-tissue release breaks up stubborn myofascial trigger points in the lats, deltoids, and lumbar spine, restoring natural muscular elasticity.

2. Joint Mobilization & Spinal Alignment

Gentle passive joint mobilization re-aligns restricted spinal segments and restores shoulder joint capsule mobility, reducing nerve compression and pain.

3. Dry Needling & Electrotherapy

Using localized micro-stimulation or targeted trigger-point release instantly relieves chronic muscle spasms and accelerates cellular blood flow to injured tissues.

4. Surf-Specific Functional Rehabilitation

Your therapist will guide you through tailored mobility, core stability, and rotator cuff strengthening exercises designed specifically to protect your body during paddling and pop-ups.
