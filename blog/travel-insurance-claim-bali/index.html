How to Claim Travel Insurance for Villa Doctor Visits in Bali

Published by Dripwell Medical Team • June 2026 • 5 min read

Falling ill while on vacation can quickly turn a dream trip into a stressful experience. Whether you are dealing with severe dehydration from Bali Belly, a sudden tropical spike requiring immediate Fever Treatment, or acute body exhaustion, seeking prompt medical attention is essential.

Fortunately, most international travel insurance policies (such as Cover-More, World Nomads, Allianz, and AXA) cover home doctor call-outs and private IV therapy treatments in Bali—provided you follow the correct claim procedures. Here is a step-by-step guide to claiming your travel insurance seamlessly for villa medical services.

1. Understand How Medical Claims Work in Bali

Travel insurance providers generally operate under two payment models when dealing with private medical care in Indonesia:

  • Pay and Claim (Reimbursement): This is the most common method for mobile healthcare and villa doctor services. You settle the medical bill upfront with the attending medical team. Afterward, you submit the official medical documentation and itemized receipts to your insurer to receive a 100% reimbursement.
  • Direct Billing (Guarantee of Payment / GOP): Typically reserved for major hospital admissions or specialized clinics where the insurer issues a direct Guarantee of Payment letter prior to treatment.

2. Essential Documents Needed for a Successful Claim

To ensure your reimbursement request is approved without delays, your insurance provider requires specific, official medical documentation. Before our medical team leaves your villa or hotel room, ensure you receive the following:

  1. Signed Medical Report & Certificate: Prepared by a licensed doctor detailing your symptoms, physical examination findings, official diagnosis, and administered treatments.
  2. Itemized Official Invoice & Receipt: Breaking down the charges including doctor consultation fees, nursing services, specific medications, and IV therapy fluids.
  3. Prescription Records: A detailed list of prescribed or administered medications (such as antibiotics, antipyretics, or anti-nausea drugs).
  4. Diagnostic Test Results: Official copies of blood work or rapid diagnostic tests (such as Dengue NS1 tests) if performed during your consultation.

3. Step-by-Step Guide to Filing Your Claim

Step 1: Request an Official Insurance Package

When booking a doctor visit through DripWell Bali Home Doctor Service, inform our team that you plan to submit a travel insurance claim. Our English-speaking doctors and nurses format every report and receipt to meet international insurance standards.

Step 2: Notify Your Insurance Provider Early

Contact your insurer’s 24/7 emergency assistance helpline via email or phone as soon as your condition is stabilized. Inform them of your location, symptoms, and that a licensed mobile doctor provided home treatment.

Step 3: Keep Digital and Physical Copies

Take clear photos or digital scans of all signed medical forms, receipts, and prescription sheets before sending the originals to your insurance company.

Step 4: Submit Your Claim Form

Log into your insurer’s online claims portal or fill out their PDF claim document. Attach your flight details (proving your travel dates) alongside the medical documents provided by DripWell Bali.

Yes, most comprehensive travel insurance policies reimburse mobile IV vitamin and hydration treatments—such as our Flu Relief Package or Jetlag Recovery Drip—as long as a licensed doctor determines the treatment is medically necessary.

If your recovery requires ongoing physical therapy—such as post-surf injury treatment—documents from our Mobile Physiotherapy Service can also be compiled for secondary medical claims.
