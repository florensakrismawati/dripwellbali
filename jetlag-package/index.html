Travel Safety Advisory

When to Seek Additional Medical Attention

While jetlag IV therapy handles standard travel fatigue, seek immediate medical help if you experience:

  • Severe calf swelling, redness, or pain (signs of DVT)
  • Chest pain or sudden shortness of breath
  • Persistent high fever or chills post-flight
  • Severe prolonged gastrointestinal illness
