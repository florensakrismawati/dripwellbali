Questions About IV Drip in Seminyak

Is IV drip therapy suitable for everyone?

No. A clinician must review your health, symptoms, medical history and relevant risks before deciding whether an infusion is appropriate. IV therapy may not be necessary or suitable for every person.

Can IV drip therapy replace medical treatment?

No. IV drip therapy is not a substitute for diagnosis or appropriate treatment. If you are unwell, the clinician will assess your situation and advise on suitable next steps.

Can you visit every address in Seminyak?

Not necessarily. Enquiries for Seminyak, Petitenget, Kerobokan, Umalas, Batu Belig, Double Six Beach and nearby parts of Seminyak are checked individually. Availability depends on the exact address, service requested and team schedule.

What should I do if I have severe symptoms?

Do not wait for a mobile visit if you have severe or rapidly worsening symptoms, fainting, confusion, difficulty breathing, chest pain or signs of severe dehydration. Seek urgent medical care.
