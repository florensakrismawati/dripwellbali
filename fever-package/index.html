Understanding Your Symptoms

Common Fever Symptoms We Treat

High body temperature rapidly drains fluids and leads to severe physical discomfort. Our mobile medical team delivers targeted antipyretic medication and IV electrolytes to lower fever fast and restore balance.

◌

High Body Temperature

Elevated core temperature, hot flashes, and intense sweating caused by viral or bacterial infection.

✦

Severe Body Aches

Deep muscle soreness, joint stiffness, and painful headaches triggered by inflammatory response.

⌖

Chills & Shivering

Uncontrollable shivering and cold sensation as your body temperature fluctuates rapidly.

✦

Dehydration & Dizziness

Fluid depletion, dry mouth, lightheadedness, and extreme weakness from fever-induced sweating.
