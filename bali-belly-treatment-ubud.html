Questions About Bali Belly Visits in Ubud

Which parts of Ubud can you cover?

Requests may be checked for Ubud and nearby areas listed on this page, including Penestanan, Sayan, Kedewatan and Mas. Coverage is not guaranteed for every address; contact us with your location to confirm.

What information should I send on WhatsApp?

Send your villa or hotel name, Google Maps pin or full address, the reason for your enquiry and current symptoms if any, your preferred visit time, and relevant medical history or known medication allergies.

How are mobile service requests confirmed?

Mobile service requests are confirmed individually based on your exact address, clinical assessment and team availability. A WhatsApp enquiry is a request only; please wait for the team to confirm your visit.

Can I request a visit if I am staying in a hotel or villa?

You can enquire about a visit to your accommodation. Please include the property name and any access instructions needed to reach you. Availability is confirmed individually.

When should I seek urgent medical care instead?

Seek urgent medical care for severe or worsening abdominal pain, blood in stool or vomit, fainting, confusion, inability to keep fluids down, very little urine or signs of severe dehydration. Do not wait for a mobile visit if symptoms may be an emergency.
