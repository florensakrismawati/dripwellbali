Understanding Your Symptoms

What is Bali Belly?

Bali Belly is a common term used by travellers to describe gastrointestinal illness, often involving diarrhea, nausea, vomiting and stomach discomfort.

◌

Diarrhea & Nausea

Frequent watery stools accompanied by constant feeling of sickness.

✦

Vomiting & Cramps

Inability to keep fluids down and sharp abdominal cramps.

⌖

Dehydration & Weakness

Rapid fluid loss leading to extreme fatigue, dizziness, and low energy.

✦

Loss of Appetite

Abdominal discomfort making it difficult to eat or drink normally.

Our treatment is supportive care designed to help with rehydration and symptom relief based on a doctor's medical assessment.

Important Safety Advisory

When You Need Hospital Care

Seek urgent emergency medical attention at a hospital if you experience any of the following severe symptoms:

  • Severe or worsening abdominal pain
  • Blood in stool or vomit
  • Persistent, uncontrollable vomiting
  • Severe dehydration & inability to pass urine
  • Fainting, dizziness, or confusion
  • High or persistent fever
  • Severe weakness & inability to stand
  • Symptoms that are rapidly getting worse

Your safety comes first. If your condition requires a higher level of medical care, our team may recommend immediate evaluation at an appropriate hospital facility.
