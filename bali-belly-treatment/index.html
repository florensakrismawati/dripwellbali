What is Bali Belly?

When your
stomach says
no.

“Bali Belly” is a common term travellers use for gastrointestinal symptoms that may occur while travelling, including diarrhea, nausea, vomiting, stomach discomfort and dehydration.

Symptoms can be uncomfortable and may interfere with your holiday. Instead of travelling to a clinic while feeling unwell, you can contact Dripwell Bali for a home doctor visit at your hotel or villa.

A doctor will assess your condition and determine the appropriate medical treatment based on your symptoms and medical needs.

01 — Common symptoms

Recognise what
your body is saying.

01

Diarrhea

Frequent or loose bowel movements can result in fluid and electrolyte loss, especially in Bali's warm climate.

02

Nausea

Persistent nausea may make it difficult to eat, drink and enjoy your holiday normally.

03

Vomiting

Repeated vomiting can contribute to dehydration and may require medical assessment.

04

Stomach pain

Abdominal discomfort, cramps or stomach aches can occur alongside gastrointestinal illness.

05

Weakness

Fluid loss and reduced food intake may leave you feeling tired, weak or unable to continue normal activities.

06

Dehydration

Loss of fluids and electrolytes can become significant, particularly when diarrhea or vomiting continues.

Important medical information

Your safety
comes first.

Bali Belly can have different causes and severity. Not every traveller requires an IV drip or medication. Our medical team assesses each patient individually.

Seek urgent medical care if symptoms are severe. Severe dehydration, fainting, confusion, blood in stool, persistent severe abdominal pain or other serious symptoms may require urgent medical evaluation.
Children and vulnerable patients Young children, older adults, pregnant patients and people with significant medical conditions may require additional medical assessment.
IV therapy is not automatically required. IV fluids and other treatments are provided only when considered medically appropriate following assessment.
