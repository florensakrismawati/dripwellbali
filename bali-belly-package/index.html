Understanding Your Symptoms

What is Bali Belly?

Bali Belly is a common form of gastroenteritis caused by consuming food or water contaminated with foreign bacteria. Our medical IV therapy provides direct-to-bloodstream relief for fast absorption.

◌

Diarrhea & Nausea

Frequent watery stools accompanied by a constant feeling of severe stomach sickness.

✦

Vomiting & Cramps

Inability to keep oral fluids or meals down, along with painful abdominal muscle spasms.

⌖

Dehydration & Fatigue

Rapid electrolyte loss leading to extreme exhaustion, dizziness, dry mouth, and muscle weakness.

✦

Loss of Appetite

Abdominal discomfort and acid build-up making oral intake impossible without medical support.

Safety First Advisory

When You Need Emergency Hospital Care

Mobile IV drip care is suitable for mild to moderate gastroenteritis. Seek immediate hospital emergency room evaluation if you experience:

  • Severe, unmanageable abdominal pain
  • Blood in your stool or vomit
  • Continuous vomiting with inability to keep any IV fluids
  • Complete inability to pass urine for over 12 hours
  • Fainting, confusion, or severe disorientation
  • Persistent high fever (>39°C / 102°F) uncommunicative to medicine

Our visiting doctor will immediately advise and assist in hospital transfer if your vital signs indicate emergency hospitalization is required.
