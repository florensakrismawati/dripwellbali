Safety First Advisory

When You Need Emergency Hospital Care

Seek immediate hospital emergency room evaluation if you experience:

  • Severe, unmanageable abdominal pain
  • Blood in your stool or vomit
  • Continuous vomiting with fluid intolerance
  • Inability to pass urine (>12 hours)
  • Fainting or severe disorientation
  • Persistent high fever (>39°C / 102°F)
